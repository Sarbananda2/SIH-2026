# Market Research

Existing systems and products that address this problem or one next to it.

Suitable material:

- Deployed systems and commercial products, and what they actually do
- Prior hackathon and academic attempts at the same problem statement
- Gaps: what nobody handles well, and why that gap exists
- Where our approach differs, stated honestly

Judges ask "how is this different from what exists?" — this directory should
contain the evidence for that answer. Cite sources for every claim about another
system's capability.
