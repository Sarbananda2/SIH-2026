## Unconfirmed — and it changes everything

Third-party mirrors of the SIH portal state the sponsoring **department is
Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), Police-II Division**, and that the full description
asks for **face detection, ANPR, and vehicle classification**.

**Neither claim is confirmed on sih.gov.in.** The master catalogue above carries
no department field and no real description — its "Overview" merely repeats the
title. So this rests on two third-party mirrors only. See
[`domain-context.md`](domain-context.md) §0 for the sources.

This matters more than any other open item in the repository:

- SSB guards the **India–Nepal and India–Bhutan borders**, which are **open and
  unfenced**, and lawfully crossed every day.
- If that is right, a fenced-perimeter "any human is an intruder" product is
  aimed at the wrong border and the wrong force.

verbatim description, organisation, and department below. Until then, treat the
