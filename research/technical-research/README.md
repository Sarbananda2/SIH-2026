# Technical Research

Evaluations of things we might build with, before we commit to them.

Suitable material:

- Model and algorithm comparisons, with the metrics that matter to us
- Library, framework, and service evaluations
- Hardware and deployment-target constraints
- Spikes and feasibility experiments, including ones that failed

Record what you rejected and why. A dead end that is written down only costs the
team once; an undocumented one gets rediscovered by the next person.

Conclusions that change the build belong in [`../../docs/decisions/`](../../docs/decisions/).
